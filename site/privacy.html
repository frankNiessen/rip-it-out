Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026 · applies to the Rip It Out apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, and this website

In short: Rip It Out has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Your songs, recordings and settings stay on your devices and in the storage you choose. Nothing is collected by the maker of Rip It Out.

What the apps store

Everything Rip It Out makes (separated tracks, click tracks, your recordings and videos, exports) is saved in the library folder you choose, on your computer or in a cloud folder of your own (for example iCloud Drive, Nextcloud or Dropbox). Settings are saved on the device. The maker of Rip It Out never receives any of it.

Microphone and camera

The apps use the microphone (or your audio interface) and, if you switch video on, the camera only while you record or calibrate on the Record page. What they capture is saved as a take in your library. It is not sent anywhere, and nothing is listened to or analysed other than to line your take up with the song.

Connections the apps make

The apps only connect to other services for something you asked for:

  • YouTube (desktop app): when you add a song from a YouTube link, to download its audio.
  • Model publishers (desktop app): the first time a model is needed, it is downloaded from its publisher (Meta for Demucs, JKU Linz for beat_this, Hugging Face for the section model).
  • PyPI (desktop app): only when you choose Update YouTube Downloader.
  • GitHub (desktop app): only when you check for updates.
  • Your Nextcloud server (iPhone and iPad): if you connect one, the app reads your library from it and uploads your takes to it. Your user name and app password are kept in the iPhone's Keychain and sent only to that server.

These services have their own privacy policies and see the usual connection details (such as your IP address) when the app connects to them.

Diagnostics

The iPhone and iPad app keeps a diagnostics log on the device (timing of takes, audio changes, uploads). It never leaves the device unless you share it yourself from Settings. The desktop app keeps its log on your computer. There is no crash reporting or usage tracking.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records technical information such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. To show the newest version on the download buttons, the page asks GitHub's public API for the latest release. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or trackers.

If you follow the tip link, the tipping service handles your payment under its own privacy policy.

Children

Rip It Out collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
