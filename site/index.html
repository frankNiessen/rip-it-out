Features

Everything a band would give you, minus your part

Separate

Four tracks per song

Drums, bass, vocals and everything else, each on its own fader with mute and solo. Two styles: Band for rock, pop and funk, Electronic for drum & bass, EDM and heavy synth bass.

drumsbassvocalsotherclick
Listen

Beats, bars and sections

Beat and bar tracking, a click on the real beats (audio and MIDI) that follows the band even where the tempo drifts, and the song's intro, verses and choruses.

Practice

Play along

A big bar and beat counter, a tempo view, a count-in of one or two bars, and loops for a section or any bars, to work on the hard part until it sits.

Record

Record yourself

From any audio interface: a guitar or bass, a microphone, or an electronic drum kit over USB, on camera too. A short calibration lines your take up with the song to the millisecond.

Review

Hear how you did

Your take next to the band, with a timing nudge. Export the whole take, a trimmed part or a loop as WAV or MP4 video, mixed the way you like.

Library

Plain folders, your cloud

Songs live in a folder you choose, as ordinary audio files. Put it in iCloud Drive, Nextcloud or Dropbox and practise on your iPhone too. Everything is processed on your computer.

How it works

From a link to your first take in a few minutes

  1. Choose a library folder

    In Settings. Everything the app makes lives there, one folder per song.

  2. Add songs

    Drop in your own MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A and more, or paste a video link. Press Process: one to two minutes per song on an M-series Mac.

  3. Play along

    Turn your instrument down, set the count-in, press Play or the space bar. Click a section to loop it.

  4. Calibrate once

    In Record > Setup, choose your interface and play along with 20 clicks. That measures your setup's delay.

  5. Record and review

    The count-in plays, the song starts, your take is saved. Listen back, nudge the timing, export audio or video.

Install

The builds aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so the first start needs one extra step.

Mac

  1. Download RipItOut-<version>.dmg, open it and drag Rip It Out to Applications.
  2. macOS blocks the first start ("damaged" or "can't be opened"). Run this once in Terminal: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Rip It Out.app"
  3. Start Rip It Out. The first song downloads the separation, beat and section models (about 410 MB, once).

Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 13 Ventura or newer · about 1.1 GB, plus about 35 MB per song. Updates install from Settings > Updates.

Windows preview

  1. Download RipItOut-Setup-<version>.exe and run it. It installs for your user, no administrator rights needed.
  2. SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. With an NVIDIA graphics card, keep GPU acceleration ticked (about 2.6 GB): separation is much faster on the GPU. Without it, Rip It Out uses the CPU.

Installer about 400 MB · app about 1.5 GB · to update, install the new version over the old one.

iPhone and iPad · preview

Practise anywhere

The iOS app plays the songs of your library and records takes, with the same mixer, count-in, sections, loops and calibration, on video too. It reads the library from Nextcloud or any folder in the Files app, and takes recorded on the phone show up on the desktop and the other way round.

Adding and separating songs stays on the computer. There is no App Store build yet: you install it with Xcode on a Mac.

Install the iPhone app with Xcode ↗

What the phone does

  1. Practice: play along with a Band fader, mute and solo per track, a count-in and section loops.
  2. Record: from the built-in microphone, a headset or a USB interface, with the camera if you like.
  3. Takes: watch and listen back, share as audio or video, or delete. Uploaded to your library in the background.

Help

Troubleshooting

Adding a song from a link fails
The video site probably changed something. Check for a newer release first; if there is none, Library > Update YouTube Downloader fetches the newest downloader (Reset YouTube Downloader undoes it). Your own files always work.
The drums took the bass and synths along
Typical for electronic music added with the Band style. Tick the songs and choose Redo separation as Electronic.
My recording is silent
Check the input in Record > Setup (the level meter should move when you play) and allow microphone access in your system's privacy settings.
My playing sits early or late
Run Calibrate again, or adjust the take's Timing.
Something else
Library > Show Log, and open an issue on GitHub.

Privacy

Stays on your computer

There is no Rip It Out server. Your songs, tracks and recordings are processed and stored on your computer. The app only goes online when you add a song from a link, the first time it needs a model, and when you check for updates. Privacy policy
