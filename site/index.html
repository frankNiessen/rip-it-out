Practice tool for musicians

Take a song apart.

Add a track to your library. Rip It Out splits it into drums, bass, vocals and everything else, finds every beat, bar and section, and builds a click that follows the band. Then mute your instrument and play along, record yourself on audio and video, and share your take with your friends.

How it works

From a track to your first take in a few minutes

  1. Choose a library folder

    In Settings. Everything the app makes lives there, one folder per song.

  2. Add songs

    Drop in your own MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A and more, or paste a video link. Press Process: one to two minutes per song on an M-series Mac.

  3. Play along

    Mute your instrument, set the count-in, press Play or the space bar. Click a section to loop it.

  4. Calibrate once

    In Record > Setup, choose your interface and play along with 20 clicks. That measures your setup's delay.

  5. Record and review

    The count-in plays, the song starts, your take is saved. Listen back, nudge the timing, export audio or video.
