Mute your part.
Play it yourself.

Add a song to your library and Rip It Out splits it into drums, bass, vocals and the rest, with a click that follows the band. Mute your instrument, play along, record yourself on audio and video, and share the take with your friends.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Free. For Macs with an Apple chip and for Windows. How to install

Any song, in four parts

Drop in a song and a minute or two later it's drums, bass, vocals and everything else, each with its own fader. Rip It Out also finds the bars and the song's parts (verse, chorus, bridge) and builds a click that follows the band, even when they speed up.

Play along until it sits

Mute your instrument and the band plays without you. A count-in gets you in, a big counter tells you where you are, and a tap on the chorus loops it as often as you need.

Record it. Share it.

Plug in your guitar, bass, microphone or electronic drums and record yourself, on camera too. Listen back with the band, then send the take to your friends as audio or video. On your phone as well, with the mobile app.
