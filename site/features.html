Features

Everything a band would give you, minus your part

Separate

Four tracks per song

Drums, bass, vocals and everything else, each on its own fader with mute and solo. Two styles: Band for rock, pop and funk, Electronic for drum & bass, EDM and heavy synth bass.

drumsbassvocalsotherclick
Listen

Beats, bars and sections

Beat and bar tracking, a click on the real beats (audio and MIDI) that follows the band even where the tempo drifts, and the song's intro, verses and choruses.

Practice

Play along

A big bar and beat counter, a tempo view, a count-in of one or two bars, and loops for a section or any bars, to work on the hard part until it sits.

Record

Record yourself

From any audio interface: a guitar or bass, a microphone, or an electronic drum kit over USB, on camera too. A short calibration lines your take up with the song to the millisecond.

Review

Hear how you did

Your take next to the band, with a timing nudge. Export the whole take, a trimmed part or a loop as WAV or MP4 video, mixed the way you like.

Library

Plain folders, your cloud

Songs live in a folder you choose, as ordinary audio files. Put it in iCloud Drive, Nextcloud or Dropbox and practise on your phone too. Everything is processed on your computer.
