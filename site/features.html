What Rip It Out does

Everything a band would give you, minus your part.

Four parts per song
Drums, bass, vocals and everything else, each with its own fader, mute and solo. A second style for electronic music keeps synth basses out of the drums.
Bars, parts and a click
The song's bars and its parts (intro, verse, chorus) are found for you, and the click follows the band even when the tempo moves.
Play along
A count-in, a big bar counter, and loops for the chorus or any bars you want to work on.
Record yourself
From your guitar or bass, a microphone or electronic drums, on camera too. A short calibration puts your take exactly in time with the song.
Listen back and share
Hear your take with the band, nudge it if needed, and share the whole take, a part or a loop as audio or video, mixed the way you like.
Your library, your devices
Songs live in a folder you choose. Put it in iCloud Drive, Nextcloud or Dropbox and practise on your phone too. Nothing leaves your computer otherwise.
